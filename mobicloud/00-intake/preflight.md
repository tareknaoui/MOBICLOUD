# Pre-Flight Check

**Project:** mobicloud
**Date:** 2026-06-21

## Dominant Incumbent Scan

**Hivenet** (Swiss, founded 2022) — Android app on Google Play performing distributed storage via device node fragmentation. Closest structural analog found. Distinctions: no Africa focus, no sovereignty/localization angle, no offline local-group operation, EU-based servers. Funded but not dominant. [Data, Crunchbase / Google Play store listing]

**Cubbit** (Italian) — B2B geo-distributed sovereign storage. Uses DS3 Composer platform targeting enterprises, not consumer Android. No Africa presence. [Data, company website / Crunchbase]

**Assessment:** No consumer-grade, phone-to-phone, trusted-group, crypto-free, Africa-focused P2P storage app found. The specific niche appears genuinely unoccupied. [Opinion]

## Precedent Failure Scan

No documented failure of a P2P mobile storage startup found. Academic projects (Blackbox, MIT 2014) attempted the concept but never commercialized. The absence of a failure graveyard means the concept is under-explored at consumer level — validating the gap but removing one source of learning about what doesn't work. [Data, web search]

## Regulatory / Legal Scan

**Strong tailwind:**
- Law No. 11-25 (July 2025) — modernized Algerian data protection framework. Introduces DPO requirements, breach notification timelines, DPIA obligations.
- Presidential Decree No. 25-321 (Dec 2025) — National Cybersecurity Strategy 2025–2029 for public administrations.
- Presidential Decree No. 26-07 (Jan 2026) — operational cybersecurity framework for public institutions.

Algeria's data sovereignty posture is backed by active legislation, not just policy rhetoric.

**Risk:**
- Algeria's 2018 law prohibits data exports threatening "public security." Relay server on Render (US-based) routes data through US infrastructure. Even with end-to-end encryption, this may conflict with institutional procurement requirements. Must host relay in-country or in an EU-equivalent jurisdiction before pursuing public sector contracts.

## Verdict

No instantly disqualifying signal. Proceed to intake.

**Immediate structural risk to address:** relay server geographic location before B2G sales.

---

## Flags

**Red Flags:**
- None identified at pre-flight stage.

**Yellow Flags:**
- Hivenet exists and has an Android app — differentiation must be explicit and defensible.
- Relay server on US infrastructure conflicts with Algerian data localization requirements for institutional clients.
- No P2P mobile storage startup has succeeded at consumer scale — absence of precedent, not evidence of feasibility.
